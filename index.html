How to Play

Single Notes: the game plays one note. Tap the matching piano key. You get three tries per level and each level lasts 30 seconds.

Chord Mode: the game plays a chord. Tap every key in that chord, then press Lock Answer. Correct chord tones turn green as you find them. Wrong keys flash red right away. Chord levels last 45 seconds.

  • Replay Sound: hear the current challenge again.
  • Guide: turns on a visual clue so the note or chord flashes blue while it plays. Leave it off for full ear-training.
  • Traffic: toggles faint downtown ambience.
  • Clear: clears your chord selection before locking.

Level 3: single-note mode uses the full keybed. Chord mode introduces major 7, minor 9, 9th, and neo-soul color shapes.

Practice Lab Help

  • Sound Bank: choose Rhodes, Grand Piano, or Guitar.
  • Two Rows: top row is lower octaves; bottom row continues into higher octaves.
  • Collapse: turns the practice area into one larger keyboard bed, about double the default row height.
  • Octave +/-: moves the whole keyboard range by octaves.
  • Transpose +/-: shifts the played note names and sounds one semitone at a time.
  • Hide Octave Numbers: removes the numbers next to key names while staying playable.
  • Live Meter: shows single notes and identifies many held chord shapes in real time, including major, minor, augmented, diminished, 7th, 9th, and neo-soul colors.
