How to Play

Single Notes: the game plays one note. Nothing lights up until you make a guess. Correct taps turn opaque green with a success sound. Wrong taps turn opaque red with a miss sound. Three misses restarts the level.

Chord Mode: the game plays a chord. Tap each key you hear. Correct chord tones turn green and stay selected. Wrong taps flash red immediately. Once all tones are found, the next chord loads.

Practice: use two full playable rows. The top row is low, the second row continues higher. Use octave and transpose controls to shift the note labels and sound output in real time.

Level 3: single-note mode jumps across the full C-to-B keybed. Chord mode introduces color chords like major 7, minor 9, dominant 9, and half-diminished shapes.
