How to Play

Single Notes: the game plays one note. Tap the matching piano key. You get three tries per level and each level lasts 30 seconds.

Chord Mode: the game plays a chord. Tap every key that belongs in that chord, then press Lock Answer. You get three tries per level and each level lasts 45 seconds.

Guide Button: turn Guide on if you want a visual assist while the sound plays. When Guide is off, the game stays fully audio-based until you answer.

Level 3: single-note mode jumps across the full C-to-B keybed. Chord mode introduces color chords like major 7, minor 9, and dominant 9 shapes for a more neo-soul style challenge.
